Our Farming Practices

Free Range

Our chickens are raised in open, spacious environments where they can roam freely and live naturally.

Natural Feed

We use only natural, locally-sourced feed with no hormones or unnecessary antibiotics.

Clean Water

Our chickens have access to clean, filtered water at all times, ensuring their health and well-being.

Humane Treatment

We follow strict animal welfare standards, treating our chickens with care and respect throughout their lives.
